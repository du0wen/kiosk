# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Last updated: November 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and restrictions governing the use of Kiosk (both self-hosted instances and official hosted services). By publishing or hosting content through Kiosk, you agree to comply with this policy.

---

## 1. Prohibited Content and Activities

You may not use Kiosk to publish, host, or distribute content that involves or facilitates:

### 1.1 Phishing and Deception
- **Credential Harvesting**: Creating fake or deceptive login forms, verification prompts, or interfaces imitating banking, payment, social media, or other services.
- **Brand Impersonation**: Misleading visitors into believing content is produced, endorsed, or affiliated with an entity or individual without authorization.
- **Social Engineering**: Fraudulent claims, fake tech support, or scareware prompts.

### 1.2 Malware and Exploits
- Distributing viruses, trojans, ransomware, keyloggers, or malicious scripts.
- Exploiting browser vulnerabilities or attempting to bypass sandboxes, iframes, or Content Security Policies.

### 1.3 Harassment and Illegal Content
- Defamatory, libelous, threatening, or harassing material.
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or non-consensual sexual imagery.
- Promotion of violence, terrorism, or hate speech targeting protected groups.

### 1.4 Copyright and Intellectual Property Infringement
- Hosting unauthorized copyrighted material, proprietary software, or trade secrets without appropriate licenses.

---

## 2. Enforcement and Takedowns

- **Immediate Suspension**: We reserve the right to immediately block, disable, or delete any page or organization account found in violation of this AUP without prior notice.
- **Abuse Reporting**: Any visitor can report abusive content via the `/report` endpoint. Reports are investigated and actioned promptly.
- **Legal Compliance**: We cooperate with law enforcement authorities and comply with valid legal processes regarding illegal activities.
